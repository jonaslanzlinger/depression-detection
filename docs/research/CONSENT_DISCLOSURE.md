# IHearYou Consent and Disclosure Requirements

**Document Version:** 1.0
**Date:** January 2026
**Status:** Draft - Pending IRB Review

## 1. Overview

This document outlines the consent and disclosure requirements for the IHearYou depression screening and monitoring research system.

## 2. Key Disclosure Requirements

### 2.1 System Purpose

Participants must be informed that:

1. **Primary Purpose**: The system analyzes voice characteristics to identify features that may be associated with depression symptoms.

2. **Screening Tool**: This is a **screening and monitoring tool**, NOT a diagnostic device.

3. **Research Context**: All data collection is for research purposes to validate the correlation between audio features and depression indicators.

4. **No Medical Advice**: The system does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment recommendations, or clinical advice.

### 2.2 Data Collection

Participants must understand:

| Data Type | Collection | Storage | Retention |
|-----------|------------|---------|-----------|
| Voice recordings | Continuous when speech detected | Encrypted | 30 days |
| Extracted features | From voice recordings | Anonymized | Study duration + 5 years |
| PHQ-9 responses | Weekly self-report | Encrypted | Study duration + 5 years |
| Speaker embeddings | From enrollment | Encrypted | Study duration |
| Device metadata | Automatic | Anonymized | 90 days |

### 2.3 Multi-Occupant Household Disclosure

For households with multiple occupants:

1. **Primary Participant**: Full consent and enrollment required
2. **Other Household Members**: Must be informed that:
   - Audio capture devices are present
   - Their voices may be incidentally recorded
   - Speaker verification attempts to exclude non-enrolled speakers
   - They have the right to request device removal from common areas

**Required Notification:**
> "This household is participating in a voice-based health monitoring research study. Audio recording devices are active in [specified rooms]. Non-participant voices are filtered and not stored for analysis."

## 3. Informed Consent Elements

### 3.1 Required Consent Components

Per IRB requirements, consent must include:

1. **Study Purpose**
   - Research into voice-based depression screening
   - Validation of audio features associated with depression

2. **Procedures**
   - Continuous audio monitoring in home environment
   - Weekly PHQ-9 questionnaire completion
   - Initial enrollment voice samples
   - Optional follow-up interviews

3. **Duration**
   - Pilot study: 8 weeks
   - Validation study: 12 weeks
   - Longitudinal study: up to 12 months

4. **Risks**
   - Privacy concerns from continuous audio monitoring
   - Potential for incidental discovery of mental health concerns
   - Emotional discomfort from regular depression screening questions
   - Risk of device malfunction or data breach (mitigated by encryption)

5. **Benefits**
   - Contribution to depression screening research
   - Regular self-monitoring of mood (PHQ-9)
   - Optional: Access to study findings upon completion

6. **Confidentiality**
   - Data de-identification procedures
   - Encryption protocols
   - Data access limitations
   - Data retention and destruction timeline

7. **Voluntary Participation**
   - Right to withdraw at any time
   - No penalty for withdrawal
   - Data deletion upon withdrawal request

8. **Contact Information**
   - Principal Investigator contact
   - IRB contact for concerns
   - Emergency mental health resources

### 3.2 Additional Disclosures

#### Privacy and Recording

> "The IHearYou system continuously listens for voice activity in your home. When speech is detected, audio is captured, processed for voice characteristics, and then deleted within 30 days. Only extracted numerical features (not voice recordings) are retained long-term for research analysis."

#### Speaker Verification

> "The system uses speaker verification technology to identify your voice among others in the household. This requires an initial enrollment session where you provide 5 minutes of speech samples. Other voices detected are not analyzed for depression features but may be incidentally captured before verification occurs."

#### Depression Screening Limitations

> "This system analyzes voice features that research suggests may be associated with depression symptoms. It is NOT a diagnostic tool and cannot determine whether you have depression. Results should not replace professional mental health assessment. If you are concerned about your mental health, please contact a healthcare provider."

#### Incidental Findings

> "If the research team identifies voice patterns suggesting significant distress or safety concerns, we may contact you to provide mental health resources. See the Incidental Findings Protocol for details on how such situations are handled."

## 4. Consent Process

### 4.1 Pre-Enrollment

1. Provide written study information sheet (minimum 48 hours before consent)
2. Conduct phone/video screening for eligibility
3. Answer all participant questions
4. Assess participant comprehension

### 4.2 Enrollment Session

1. Review consent form in full
2. Verbal comprehension check (ask participant to explain key points)
3. Signature collection (electronic signature permitted)
4. Copy of consent provided to participant

### 4.3 Ongoing Consent

1. Quarterly consent reminder (for studies > 3 months)
2. Notification of any protocol changes
3. Re-consent if significant changes to data handling

## 5. Household Member Notification

### 5.1 Notification Requirements

For each household member over age 18 who is not enrolled:

1. Written notification of study participation
2. Description of audio capture devices and locations
3. Assurance that their data is not analyzed for depression
4. Contact information for questions or concerns
5. Right to request device relocation

### 5.2 Notification Form Template

```
HOUSEHOLD NOTIFICATION: VOICE MONITORING RESEARCH STUDY

Dear Household Member,

[Participant Name] is participating in the IHearYou depression screening research study. As part of this study, voice recording devices have been installed in:
- [Room 1]
- [Room 2]

Important Information:
1. Devices continuously listen for speech
2. Your voice may be incidentally recorded
3. Speaker verification technology filters out non-enrolled voices
4. Your voice data is NOT analyzed for the study
5. All recordings are encrypted and deleted within 30 days

You have the right to:
- Request device relocation from common areas
- Ask questions about the study
- Contact the research team with concerns

Contact: [Research Team Contact Info]

By remaining in this household, you acknowledge this notification.
If you have concerns, please contact us at [email/phone].
```

## 6. Minor Children in Household

### 6.1 Parental Notification

If minor children reside in the household:

1. Parent/guardian must be notified separately
2. Written acknowledgment required
3. Devices should not be placed in children's bedrooms
4. Additional privacy protections apply

### 6.2 Child Exclusion

The system must NOT:
- Analyze children's voices for depression features
- Store children's voice data beyond technical necessity
- Include children in speaker verification database

## 7. Withdrawal Procedures

### 7.1 Participant Withdrawal

Upon withdrawal request:

1. Cease all data collection within 24 hours
2. Delete raw audio within 48 hours
3. Anonymized features retained unless specific deletion requested
4. Confirm deletion in writing within 7 days

### 7.2 Household Member Objection

If a non-enrolled household member objects:

1. Relocate devices from common areas
2. Offer participant alternative monitoring options
3. If unresolvable, participant may need to withdraw

## 8. Data Access and Correction

### 8.1 Participant Rights

Participants have the right to:

1. **Access**: Request copy of their data (within 30 days)
2. **Correction**: Request correction of inaccurate data
3. **Deletion**: Request deletion of identifiable data
4. **Portability**: Receive data in standard format

### 8.2 Access Request Process

1. Submit written request to research team
2. Identity verification required
3. Response within 30 days
4. Explanation if request cannot be fulfilled

## 9. Special Circumstances

### 9.1 Capacity to Consent

If participant's capacity to consent is questioned:

1. Pause enrollment
2. Consult with clinical advisor
3. Consider legally authorized representative
4. Document decision process

### 9.2 Language Access

Consent materials must be available in:
- English (primary)
- Spanish (if applicable to study population)
- Other languages as needed for inclusive participation

## 10. Documentation

### 10.1 Required Records

- Signed consent forms (retain for 7 years minimum)
- Household notification acknowledgments
- Withdrawal requests and actions taken
- Data access requests and responses

### 10.2 Audit Trail

- Consent date and method (in-person, electronic)
- Comprehension verification completion
- Protocol version consented to
- Any modifications or re-consent events
