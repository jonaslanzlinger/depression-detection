# IHearYou Incidental Findings Protocol

**Document Version:** 1.0
**Date:** January 2026
**Status:** Draft - Pending IRB and Clinical Review

## 1. Overview

This protocol defines procedures for handling incidental findings during the IHearYou depression screening research study. Incidental findings are observations that may indicate significant health concerns but were not the primary focus of the research.

### 1.1 Scope

This protocol covers:
- Severe depression indicators
- Potential suicidal ideation markers
- Significant emotional distress
- Audio content suggesting safety concerns

### 1.2 Key Principle

> "Participant safety takes precedence over research objectives. When safety concerns arise, research activities may be paused to ensure appropriate care is provided."

## 2. Definition of Reportable Findings

### 2.1 Category 1: Immediate Action Required

**Criteria:** Clear indication of imminent safety risk

Examples:
- Explicit verbal statement of suicidal intent in audio
- PHQ-9 Item 9 score ≥ 2 with specific ideation
- Audio content suggesting immediate danger

**Response Time:** Within 1 hour of identification

### 2.2 Category 2: Urgent Notification

**Criteria:** Significant distress requiring prompt attention

Examples:
- PHQ-9 total score ≥ 20 (severe depression)
- PHQ-9 Item 9 score = 1 (thoughts of self-harm)
- Sustained pattern of severe feature indicators
- Participant-reported crisis via study channels

**Response Time:** Within 24 hours of identification

### 2.3 Category 3: Study Monitoring

**Criteria:** Elevated indicators warranting enhanced monitoring

Examples:
- PHQ-9 total score 15-19 (moderately severe)
- Significant increase in depression features over time
- Pattern of declining engagement with study

**Response Time:** Addressed at next scheduled contact or within 7 days

## 3. Detection Procedures

### 3.1 Automated Detection

The system may flag:

| Indicator | Threshold | Category |
|-----------|-----------|----------|
| PHQ-9 Total Score | ≥ 20 | Category 2 |
| PHQ-9 Item 9 | ≥ 1 | Category 2 |
| PHQ-9 Item 9 | ≥ 2 | Escalate to Category 1 review |
| Feature deviation | > 3 SD from baseline | Category 3 |
| Engagement drop | < 30% expected data | Category 3 |

**Important:** Automated flags require human review before action.

### 3.2 Human Review

All automated flags are reviewed by:
- Research coordinator (initial review)
- Clinical advisor (Category 1 and 2 findings)
- PI (notification of all Category 1 findings)

### 3.3 Audio Content Review

**Policy:** Research staff do NOT routinely listen to participant audio.

**Exception:** Audio may be reviewed only when:
1. Automated systems detect potential safety keywords
2. Participant requests review of specific content
3. Technical issue requires audio verification

**Procedure for Audio Review:**
1. Two-person review required
2. Clinical advisor present for safety concerns
3. Documentation of review necessity
4. Immediate destruction of reviewed segments after action

## 4. Response Procedures

### 4.1 Category 1: Immediate Action Protocol

**Step 1: Initial Response (0-15 minutes)**
1. Clinical advisor notified immediately
2. Attempt to reach participant by phone
3. If no response, proceed to welfare check protocol

**Step 2: Participant Contact (15-60 minutes)**
1. Assess current safety status
2. Provide crisis resources (988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline)
3. Encourage professional evaluation
4. Document conversation

**Step 3: Escalation if Needed**
1. If participant unreachable after 4 attempts over 2 hours
2. If participant reports active suicidal plan
3. Contact emergency services for welfare check
4. Notify participant's emergency contact (if provided)

**Step 4: Documentation**
1. Complete Incidental Findings Report within 24 hours
2. IRB notification within 24-48 hours (per local requirements)
3. PI notification immediately

### 4.2 Category 2: Urgent Notification Protocol

**Step 1: Review (0-4 hours)**
1. Clinical advisor reviews finding
2. Assess severity and context
3. Determine appropriate response

**Step 2: Participant Contact (4-24 hours)**
1. Phone call to participant
2. Discuss PHQ-9 responses (if applicable)
3. Provide mental health resources
4. Recommend professional consultation
5. Offer study support options

**Step 3: Follow-up**
1. Schedule follow-up contact within 7 days
2. Increase monitoring frequency
3. Document all interactions

**Step 4: Documentation**
1. Complete Incidental Findings Report within 48 hours
2. Update participant file with enhanced monitoring flag

### 4.3 Category 3: Enhanced Monitoring Protocol

**Step 1: Internal Review (0-48 hours)**
1. Research coordinator reviews data
2. Assess trend vs. isolated finding
3. Determine if escalation needed

**Step 2: Participant Check-in (within 7 days)**
1. Routine check-in call
2. General well-being inquiry
3. Reminder of available resources
4. No disclosure of specific findings unless participant asks

**Step 3: Ongoing Monitoring**
1. Increase review frequency
2. Lower thresholds for Category 2 escalation
3. Document monitoring plan

## 5. Resources Provided to Participants

### 5.1 Standard Resources (All Participants)

Provided at enrollment and quarterly:

**Crisis Resources:**
- 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline (call or text 988)
- Crisis Text Line (text HOME to 741741)
- National Alliance on Mental Illness (NAMI) Helpline: 1-800-950-6264

**Local Resources:**
- [List of local mental health services]
- [Community mental health center contact]
- [Participant's primary care provider, if known]

### 5.2 Enhanced Resources (Category 2 Findings)

Additional resources provided:
- Direct referral to mental health provider (if participant consents)
- List of therapists accepting new patients
- Information on telehealth options
- Study-affiliated clinical advisor contact

### 5.3 Immediate Resources (Category 1 Findings)

In addition to above:
- Direct connection to crisis services
- Offer to stay on line until help arrives
- Emergency contact notification (if authorized)

## 6. Documentation Requirements

### 6.1 Incidental Findings Report

Required elements:
1. Participant ID (no PII)
2. Date and time of finding
3. Category classification
4. Description of finding
5. Source of finding (automated, PHQ-9, other)
6. Actions taken
7. Participant response
8. Follow-up plan
9. Staff involved

### 6.2 Retention

- Reports retained for study duration + 7 years
- Stored separately from research data
- Access limited to PI, clinical advisor, IRB

## 7. Staff Training Requirements

### 7.1 Required Training

All staff must complete:
1. Human subjects research training (CITI)
2. Suicide risk assessment basics
3. Crisis intervention overview
4. Study-specific incidental findings protocol

### 7.2 Clinical Advisor Qualifications

- Licensed mental health professional (PhD, LCSW, or equivalent)
- Experience with depression assessment
- Available for consultation within 4 hours (Category 2) or 1 hour (Category 1)

## 8. Special Considerations

### 8.1 After-Hours Findings

For findings detected outside business hours:

**Category 1:**
- On-call clinical advisor notified immediately
- Full protocol followed regardless of time

**Category 2:**
- Documented and flagged for morning review
- Unless PHQ-9 Item 9 ≥ 2, then treat as Category 1

**Category 3:**
- Addressed next business day

### 8.2 Participant Refusal of Help

If participant declines assistance:
1. Document refusal
2. Remind of voluntary participation
3. Provide resources in writing
4. Establish follow-up contact time
5. Escalate if imminent danger suspected despite refusal

### 8.3 Participant Withdrawal After Finding

If participant wishes to withdraw after incidental finding:
1. Process withdrawal per standard protocol
2. Provide resources before completing withdrawal
3. Offer single follow-up contact option
4. Document that resources were provided

## 9. IRB Reporting

### 9.1 Reportable Events

Report to IRB:
- All Category 1 findings (within 24-48 hours)
- Category 2 findings resulting in hospitalization (within 7 days)
- Aggregate Category 2/3 findings (quarterly)

### 9.2 Serious Adverse Events

If incidental finding relates to serious adverse event:
- Follow institutional SAE reporting procedures
- May require study pause pending review

## 10. Quality Assurance

### 10.1 Protocol Review

- Review protocol annually
- Update based on findings and best practices
- Clinical advisor input required for changes

### 10.2 Case Review

- Quarterly review of all incidental findings
- Assessment of response appropriateness
- Identification of protocol improvements

### 10.3 Metrics Tracking

Track and report:
- Number of findings by category
- Response time compliance
- Participant outcomes (if available)
- Staff training completion
